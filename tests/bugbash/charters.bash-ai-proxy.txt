# Charters for the bash AI proxy branch. Run with BUGBASH_AI=mock BUGBASH_SCENARIO=bash-ai-proxy and
# BUGBASH_ARGS="--charters tests/bugbash/charters.bash-ai-proxy.txt" (see e2e.config.ts).
# Keep explorers out of Terminal tabs: they run real shell commands on the host.
proxy-costs|web|skeptic|Turn on Settings > Providers > 'Count AI calls from bash commands', then in the 'Bug bash playground' workspace send 'probe', '[bash:many]' and '[bash:openai]'; after each, cross-check the token and cost numbers printed by the bash command against the Stats > Cost tab (per-model rows, components, session total, Last Request) and Analytics, and report every number that does not add up
proxy-switch|web|state|In Settings > Providers, find the 'Bash commands' switch and toggle it on and off, reloading after each change; after each state send 'probe' in the 'Bug bash playground' workspace and check that the proxy variables appear only while the switch is on, that the switch state persists, and that the Cost tab never loses or double-counts earlier spend
proxy-live|web|skeptic|Turn on the bash AI proxy switch, open the Stats > Cost tab of the 'Bug bash playground' workspace, then send '[bash:background]' and watch the Cost tab for 40 seconds without reloading; then turn the switch off while the background command still runs; report totals that do not grow as calls happen, grow too much, change after a reload, or keep growing after the switch is off
proxy-inputs|web|fuzzer|Turn on the bash AI proxy switch, then in the 'Bug bash playground' workspace send '[bash:stream]', '[bash:bad-key]', '[bash:openai]' and messages that mix several keywords, unicode and 300 characters; report wrong or missing usage, errors shown as success, a stuck chat, or a wrong key that still gets counted
proxy-workspaces|web|state|Turn on the bash AI proxy switch, create a second workspace in demo-app, and send 'probe' and '[bash:many]' in each workspace; check that each workspace's Cost tab and Analytics count only its own bash calls, that the two keys differ, and that archiving and restoring a workspace keeps its numbers
proxy-phone|phone|newcomer|On this phone-sized screen, open Settings > Providers and find the 'Bash commands' section; read its title, description and help, turn it on, then send 'probe' in the 'Bug bash playground' workspace and open the Cost tab; report copy that is confusing or wrong, and content that is cut off, overlapping or unreachable
